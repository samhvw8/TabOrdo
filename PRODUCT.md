# Product

TabOrdo - Tab Manager & Organizer

## Users

Productivity enthusiasts who accumulate 30-150+ tabs across multiple windows. They want their browser organized but don't want to fight a complex UI to get there. Context: mid-workflow, switching fast, often triggered by Cmd+E — every interaction competes with whatever they were actually doing. Speed and clarity win; anything that slows the return to real work loses.

## Product Purpose

Keyboard-first tab management for Chrome. Sort, group, deduplicate, archive, and triage tabs through a command palette and a dense dashboard. Success: the user spends less time managing tabs and more time using them. The extension should feel like it was always part of Chrome.

## Brand Personality

Calm, systematic, precise. Like a well-organized workspace — everything has a place, nothing is louder than it needs to be. The tool earns trust by being fast and predictable, not by being visually impressive.

## Anti-references

- Corporate SaaS (Salesforce, Jira) — heavy, overloaded, everything requires three clicks
- Dated browser extensions (OneTab, Tab Wrangler) — icon-heavy, cluttered, Chrome Web Store circa 2018
- Flashy/trendy UI — neon, glassmorphism, decoration over function

## Design Principles

- **Disappear into the workflow.** The best interaction is the shortest one. Get in, act, get out.
- **Density without clutter.** Show everything relevant, nothing decorative. Information density is a feature.
- **Predictable over clever.** Same action, same result, same place. No surprises.
- **Keyboard-native.** Every action reachable without a mouse. Visual affordances exist for discovery, not daily use.
- **Quiet confidence.** The UI communicates competence through restraint, not through showing off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. High contrast ratios on the dark theme. Full keyboard navigation (already core to the product). Reduced motion support for animations. Color not used as the sole differentiator — labels and icons supplement accent colors for group identification.
